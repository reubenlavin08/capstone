## THE ACTUAL RUBRIC (capstone-specific presentation rubric — not the generic CLC one)
4-point scale: Emerging / Developing / **Proficient** / Extending. Assessor circles one per criterion.

### 1. Mentorship & Networks
**Guideline: 3 mentor meetings and 2 expert consultations**
- **Emerging** — no meaningful engagement, well below guidelines, reflection absent/superficial
- **Developing** — limited engagement, below guidelines, describes conversations but doesn't show what was gained
- **Proficient** — engaged with process mentor + at least 2 subject matter experts, describes what was gained and how it informed project work
- **Extending** — rich engagement beyond guidelines, articulates how specific conversations shaped/redirected/deepened the project

### 2. Self-Representation
- **Emerging** — not structured, cluttered/illegible slides, unclear delivery, no engagement
- **Developing** — some structure, slides duplicate speech, lacks confidence/polish, inconsistent engagement
- **Proficient** — clear beginning/middle/end, slides complement (don't duplicate) speech, confident delivery, appropriate eye contact and body language
- **Extending** — persuasive and compelling, polished slides, handles Q&A with ease

### 3. Project Management
- **Emerging** — no Plan of Action shared/referenced
- **Developing** — references PoA but no evidence it guided work; unsystematic
- **Proficient** — presents PoA + demonstrates returning to and adapting it; describes how milestones/timelines shaped execution
- **Extending** — PoA central to narrative; reflects on planning decisions, course corrections, what they'd approach differently

### 4. Career-Life Research
- **Emerging** — single method/source, no curiosity, no artifacts
- **Developing** — narrow methods, thin artifacts, little exploration
- **Proficient** — multiple appropriate methods (interviews, academic research, field observation, experimentation), shares artifacts where appropriate
- **Extending** — beyond expected methods, compelling and well-integrated artifacts

### 5. Career-Life Reflection
- **Emerging** — describes what they did, not what they learned or why
- **Developing** — some content reflection, struggles with growth areas/what they'd do differently, minimal process reflection
- **Proficient** — objective accurate reflection on BOTH content learning AND process; identifies strengths, growth areas, lessons learned
- **Extending** — genuine, insightful, honest, evidence-based, forward-looking; connects to future goals and strategies
